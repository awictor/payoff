# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first release
- Debt payoff planner: month-by-month simulation of snowball vs avalanche with extra payments.
- Shows debt-free date, total interest, total paid, payoff order, and a head-to-head comparison of the two strategies.
- Dynamic debt list, dark mode, local persistence, copy-plan. 6 CI-backed tests. Single offline index.html.
